Explore Pest Control Strategies

Learn how to identify, prevent, and manage common crop pests using sustainable and effective pest control methods — including biological, organic, and integrated approaches.

1. Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

IPM combines biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical tools in a way that minimizes risks to health and the environment.

2. Biological Control

Use of natural predators like ladybugs or parasitic wasps to manage harmful insect populations.

3. Organic Pesticides

Utilize environmentally friendly pesticides derived from natural sources like neem oil, garlic spray, or pyrethrin.

4. Crop Rotation

Changing crop types seasonally to prevent pest adaptation and reduce pest population buildup.
